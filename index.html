My story

I make dance
for everybody.

I'm AFRODONKE, also known as Coach Phil and Thee Feel. I'm a Kenyan dancer, content creator, Super MC, and brand strategist based in Nairobi.

My work is about making dance and choreography accessible to everyone. Through community fitness classes and masterclasses, I bring people together to learn, move, and find their own groove.

I also work with schools, brands, and events, creating student showcases and bringing energy to every room I step into.
